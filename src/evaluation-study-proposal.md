# Evaluation of a Guardian-Signed Electronic Promissory Note Workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc.

## Status and provenance

Study B proposal, draft v0.1, created following the researcher's two-study direction on 6 November 2026. Adapted from the evaluation concerns of the combined proposal in [Study A](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-research/blob/main/src/archive/combined-study-proposal.md). This is planning, not an approved protocol or completed research.


# Aim and research questions

**Aim:** Evaluate a versioned implementation of the guardian-signed electronic promissory note workflow in confirmed participating units at Mabini Colleges, Inc., assessing operational outcomes, stakeholder experience, technical behavior, and practical limitations.

- **B-RQ1:** How do the baseline and electronic workflows compare in total request-to-decision time, completion, physical visits, printing, staff handling, and resubmission within defined observation windows?
- **B-RQ2:** How do participating students, guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel experience task usability, payment-term comprehension, and perceived trustworthiness?
- **B-RQ3:** Under specified tests, does the recorded implementation preserve signed terms, restrict access, enforce approval order, and maintain correct behavior for invalid, duplicate, stale, concurrent, terminal, and legacy cases?
- **B-RQ4:** What burdens, misunderstandings, and contextual limitations arise from guardian signing on the student's device inside the student session?

Study A supplies the design rationale and handoff. This study examines the implementation and observed outcomes; it does not assume that implementation improves performance. Findings remain local to the confirmed setting, period, and artifact version.
