# Study scope and sources

This repository concerns **Study B — implementation evaluation** at Mabini Colleges, Inc. Read `docs/study-split.md`, `docs/protocol.md`, `docs/introduction/research-questions.md`, and `docs/roadmap.md` for current questions, methods, pending decisions, and ownership. These living documents supersede the earlier combined-study scope; no approved protocol or completed research is established by their existence.

Study A: https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-research. Study B: https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation. Implementation requirements: https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/mc-portal/issues/740. Verify the current issue and tested implementation version before readiness claims. The Study A source archive is historical planning; the inventory/POS example is not promissory-note evidence.

The proposed artifact retains guardian signing on the student's device inside the student session, preserved signed terms, sequential Dean/President approvals, existing policies, and legacy compatibility. It does not independently authenticate the guardian or establish cryptographic signing or legal enforceability. Guardian accounts, external signing links, OTP, external signature providers, and retroactive legacy conversion remain excluded.

Study A owns needs, requirements, mockups, and formative design validation; Study B owns implementation verification and comparative/participant evaluation. Study B's approved baseline precedes rollout. Study A process observations are not automatically a comparable baseline. All claims remain bounded to confirmed local units and evidence. Approval speed does not establish repayment, retention, or academic benefits.
