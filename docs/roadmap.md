# Study B roadmap

Track the work in the [Study B GitHub roadmap](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation/issues/1). All research activities below are planned. No collection, testing, approval, or implementation is established by this foundation.

1. **Protocol and access:** Finalize scope, participants, sample rationale, primary outcome, follow-up, approvals/consent, storage, and analysis choices. Verify the decision register in the [protocol](protocol.md).
2. **Instruments and measurement:** Validate source/event availability, comparison definitions, role-specific tasks, comprehension scoring, and validated instrument use. Dry-run with synthetic records.
3. **Pre-rollout baseline:** Collect the approved baseline before the existing process changes. Record context, coverage, and denominators; amend the protocol if a prospective comparison becomes impossible.
4. **Artifact verification:** Receive the versioned [Study A handoff](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-research) and verify the actual [#740 implementation](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/mc-portal/issues/740) against all criteria, including migration and regressions. Record commit/environment and failures.
5. **Pilot and field evaluation:** After applicable readiness decisions, pilot and collect the post-rollout measurements and feedback; retain deviations and defect dispositions.
6. **Reproducible analysis:** Apply the prespecified pending-aware comparative analysis, role-specific participant assessment, technical evidence synthesis, and qualitative coding. Publish only permitted sanitized materials.
7. **Manuscript:** Answer B-RQ1–B-RQ4 with evidence and uncertainty; report neutral/negative results, confounding, identity limits, and practical burdens.

Protocol/instrument preparation and the baseline must not wait for completed implementation. New-workflow pilot/field collection depends on actual artifact readiness and Study A's versioned handoff. Maintain the [study split](study-split.md) and disclose evidence reuse.
