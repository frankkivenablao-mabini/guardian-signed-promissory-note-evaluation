# Background and evaluation problem

[Study A](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-research) will establish the local needs and design of the guardian-signed promissory note workflow. [mc-portal #740](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/mc-portal/issues/740) proposes signing on the student's device before Dean and President's Office approvals, with preserved signed terms and legacy compatibility.

Requirements do not establish performance or benefit. Study B will examine whether the recorded implementation behaves as specified and how operational outcomes and participant experiences compare under defined conditions. Measure possible guardian/device burden alongside reduced paper handoffs. Local process descriptions and baselines must be verified rather than inferred from the issue or a prior template.
