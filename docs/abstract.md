# Proposed abstract

This proposed study will evaluate a guardian-signed electronic promissory note workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc. The design basis will be supplied by a separate requirements and design study, with implementation tracked in mc-portal. The evaluation will combine versioned technical verification, an approved prospective baseline/post-rollout comparison where feasible, observed tasks, and participant feedback. Outcomes will include total request-to-decision time including guardian waiting, completion, physical visits, printing, staff handling, resubmission, usability, comprehension, and practical burdens. Technical evidence will assess signed-term preservation, authorized access, approval order, concurrency, and legacy compatibility under specified tests. Candidate participants are eligible students, participating guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel in confirmed units. Scope, sample, dates, permissions, instruments, and analysis choices require protocol finalization before affected collection. Student-session signing does not independently authenticate the guardian. Findings will be bounded to the local setting and tested implementation; nonrandomized comparisons will require careful treatment of context and uncertainty. No findings or demonstrated benefits are reported.
