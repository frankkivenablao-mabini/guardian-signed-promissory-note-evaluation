# Research design

Plan a mixed-method, single-institution evaluation with synthetic technical tests, a prospective nonrandomized baseline/post-rollout comparison where feasible, task observations, and qualitative feedback. Initial methodological sources [1–2] provide contribution/evaluation-planning context, not local evidence. The [protocol](../protocol.md) defines dependencies and unresolved decisions.

Keep research requests, participants, technical cases, and interview evidence as distinct units. Record artifact, protocol, and instrument versions. Resolve a justified alternative through a protocol amendment if a comparable prospective baseline is unavailable. Do not infer causation from an uncontrolled before-and-after difference.
