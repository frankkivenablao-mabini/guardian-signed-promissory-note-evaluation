# Anticipated limitations

- The study concerns confirmed Mabini Colleges, Inc. units, observation windows, and an identified implementation; generalization requires separate evidence.
- Nonrandomized comparisons may reflect workload, calendar, staffing, policy, or request-mix changes; legacy requests are not automatically equivalent controls.
- Pending, cancelled, repeated, and missing observations can bias a completed-case comparison and require prespecified handling.
- Student-session signing does not independently establish guardian identity; guardian/device access can shift burden.
- Tested behavior is bounded by test coverage/version and does not establish absence of vulnerabilities or legal enforceability.
- Participation, device familiarity, accessibility, and self-report can affect observed experiences.

Approval speed does not establish repayment, retention, or academic outcomes. No observed limitation or benefit is claimed before collection.
